    Berapa harga rumah kontainer di Bali?How much is a container house in Bali?

    Apa itu bangunan kontainer lipat?What is a foldable container building?

    Bangunan prefabrikasi berkerangka baja galvanis yang dikirim terlipat (tebal sekitar 40 cm), lalu dibuka dan dipasang di lokasi.A prefabricated galvanized-steel building shipped folded (about 40 cm thick), then opened and installed on site.

    Bisa dikustom untuk kafe, kost, atau hotel kapsul?Can it be customized as a cafe, boarding house or capsule hotel?

    Seberapa mudah pemasangannya?How easy is installation?

    Tanpa alat berat khusus. Unit lipat bisa berdiri dalam hitungan jam.No special heavy equipment needed. Foldable units can stand within hours.

    Biaya pembangunan dan pengiriman lebih hemat dibanding rumah konvensional karena dibuat di pabrik dan dikirim ringkas.Construction and shipping costs are lower than a conventional house because units are factory-made and shipped compactly.
